Analyzing a Dataset:

Step 1: Load the dataset
- Obtain the necessary dataset.
- Open the analysis program of choice
- Import the dataset
- Begin to review data (observations, variables, etc.)

Step 2: Clean the data (explain how)
- Begin to check for missing values, duplicates, outliers, or incorrect variables.
- Proceed with updating those values or removing them all together.

Step 3: Calculate summary statistics
- Begin to calculate the mean, median, mode, range, variance, and shape of distribution to provide context on the size and systemtry of the dataset.
- Review the counts and percentages for categorical variables

Step 4: Create a visualization
- Start to map out your numeric or categorical values into visual elements that can communicate patterns, comparisons, or distributions. Here you can use appropriate graphs like histograms, boxplots, or scatterplots. 
- Ensuring that titles and labels are properly assigned to make the visualization easy to understand.

Step 5: Interpret results
- Review the findings from the summary statistics and visualizations.
- identify important patterns, distributions, or trends that communicate what the dataset shows.
- Create a summarized review of the findings from the analysis.
